repository for which OpenKaggle contributors hold the necessary rights.

It does not grant rights to competition data, sample submissions, organizer
materials, model artifacts derived from restricted inputs, third-party models,

The locally recorded competition rules state that SZUTreeHSI data may be used
for this competition's algorithm development, training, validation, and
evaluation, and may not be published, redistributed, transferred, used
commercially, or used for unrelated research without prior written permission.

evidence. They do not include raw hyperspectral scenes or hidden labels and do
not imply affiliation with Kaggle, Google, Shenzhen University, or the
competition organizers.
